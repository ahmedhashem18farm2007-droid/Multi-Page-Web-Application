Hunter X Hunter

Hunter x Hunter is a famous Japanese anime and manga created by Yoshihiro Togashi. It follows a world full of dangerous creatures, treasures, mysteries, and powerful fighters called Hunters.

Main Character:
Gon Freecss Gon is a 12-year-old boy who discovers that his father—who he thought was dead—is actually a legendary Hunter. So Gon decides to become a Hunter himself to find him.

The Story To become a Hunter,
Gon must pass the Hunter Exam, a deadly and challenging test. During the journey, he meets three important friends: Killua Zoldyck – a skilled young assassin Kurapika – seeks revenge for his clan Leorio – wants to become a doctor Together, they face many adventures, dangerous enemies, and powerful villains.

Why Hunter x Hunter is so popular?
Amazing characters Smart fights full of strategy Emotional story arcs Deep world-building One of the best power systems (Nen)
