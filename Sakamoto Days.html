Sakamoto Days

Sakamoto Days is a Japanese manga (and upcoming anime) written by Yuto Suzuki. It’s a mix of action, comedy, and slice-of-life, about a retired hitman living a normal life.

Main Character:
Taro Sakamoto Once, Sakamoto was the deadliest hitman in the world. He retires to live a peaceful life running a small convenience store. He’s married and has gained some weight, making him look like a normal middle-aged man.

The Story
Even though he retired, Sakamoto can’t escape his past. Other assassins, gangs, and enemies keep coming after him. Despite his relaxed lifestyle, he’s still incredibly strong and defeats enemies in funny and creative ways.

Why Sakamoto Days is popular?
Combines hilarious comedy with intense action A unique take on a retired hitman story Clever fight scenes and over-the-top battles Relatable themes of balancing peaceful life and chaos
