What does anime mean?

Anime is one of the most popular forms of animation, originally created in Japan and later spreading all over the world. It is known for its unique art style, which includes large expressive eyes, detailed characters, and bright colors. Anime is not only for children; there are many genres suitable for teens and adults as well.

Today, anime has become an important part of global culture.
Millions of fans watch it, discuss it, and attend events and conventions dedicated to it. Its influence continues to inspire creativity and imagination among young people around the world.
