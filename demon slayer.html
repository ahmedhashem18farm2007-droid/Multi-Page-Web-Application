Demon Slayer

Demon Slayer (Kimetsu no Yaiba) is a popular Japanese anime and manga created by Koyoharu Gotouge. It tells the story of a world where demons exist and hunt humans, and Demon Slayers are warriors who fight them.

Main Character:
Tanjiro Kamado Tanjiro is a kind-hearted boy whose family is killed by demons. His younger sister, Nezuko, survives but turns into a demon. Tanjiro becomes a Demon Slayer to protect people and find a cure for Nezuko.

The Story
Tanjiro trains under strict masters to learn Breathing Techniques, which give him superhuman abilities. He joins the Demon Slayer Corps and faces many powerful demons, including the Twelve Kizuki, elite demon warriors under the demon king, Muzan Kibutsuji. The story is about courage, family, friendship, and fighting against evil.

Why Demon Slayer is so popular?
Stunning animation and fight scenes Emotional and heartwarming story Strong, relatable characters Unique sword techniques and powers
